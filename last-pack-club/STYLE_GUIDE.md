# The Last Pack Club style guide

Use One-Last-Pack-Vertical-Zoom.mp4 as the primary model for drawing and character scale. Its shop pairs warm ochre plaster, muted teal tiles, a glass card counter and terracotta floor with paper grain and dark ink contours. Keep the figure readable against the shelves. The Snorlax film supplies aisle depth, warm window light and shelf dressing. Its character variation is secondary.

Use the Club logo for Chase’s facial identity and brand palette. The visible logo pairs warm red, dark teal, pale cream and deep plum-brown ink with amber-orange accents. These are visual colour descriptions. An original logo file is pending. Never substitute a redraw for the brand master.

## Production defaults

Use Code Engine B, B6, 20 seconds, 1080 × 1920 portrait, native 30 fps (600 authored frames). Set single-line bottom captions in uppercase, cream or white with a dark outline. Check phone-size legibility and keep the baseline clear of important action. Use timed sound effects and no voiceover. Additional spend must remain £0. Use local tooling or an included capability with no additional charge.
